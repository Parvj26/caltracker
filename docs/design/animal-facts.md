## Facts I couldn't verify confidently

Verification note: Britannica returned 403 to my fetch tool, and several Smithsonian, National Geographic, Eiffel Tower and UK Parliament pages returned 403 or 404. Facts below were NOT checked against the cited page in this pass. They are widely documented, but the source link should be opened and confirmed before launch.

- **Corgi:** Kept only the herding and heel-nipping claim. The 'short legs dodge kicks' part appeared mainly on Wikipedia and breed sites, so I dropped it as folklore-prone. AKC link not fetched.
- **Rabbit, Goat, Sheep, Pig, Horse, Cow, Golden retriever:** Britannica or AKC links not fetched (403). Facts are standard, but unconfirmed here.
- **Kangaroo, Rhino:** Source pages returned 404, so the links are unconfirmed. The fifth-leg tail claim for kangaroos is from research on kangaroo locomotion, not seen on the cited page.
- **Axolotl:** Limb regeneration is well supported (Live Science, not a preferred tier). Critically endangered status is from search results, not the IUCN page itself.
- **Quokka:** Vulnerable status and Rottnest fox-free claim came from search results (including a DBCA document). One DBCA sheet still says Near Threatened, so confirm the current IUCN entry. I omitted population numbers.
- **Golden snub-nosed monkey:** Endangered status from search results only. The cited IUCN link is a search link, and one university page said Vulnerable, so confirm.
- **Pink fairy armadillo:** Only low-quality sources found for details. I kept a minimal fact and left rarity as a dash (IUCN reportedly Data Deficient, unconfirmed).
- **White peacock:** Only a zoo listing and low-authority sites support the 'leucistic Indian peafowl' claim. Rarity left as a dash.
- **Narwhal:** National Geographic lists Near Threatened, which I believe is outdated versus the current IUCN rating, so I left rarity as a dash.
- **Fennec fox, Snowy owl:** Rarity left as a dash because I did not retrieve their IUCN status.
- **White tiger:** Rarity line mixes NatGeo (tigers endangered) with a Smithsonian Magazine claim (last wild white tiger 1958). The 'no wild sightings since 1958' part is a historical claim, so confirm it.
- **Pink river dolphin:** Endangered status is from search results (IUCN 2018/2023 mentions), and the NatGeo page does not state it. The neck-bend fact is NatGeo-verified. Older assessments said Data Deficient, so confirm.
- **Big Ben, Eiffel Tower, Burj Khalifa, Golden Gate Bridge, Mount Fuji, Mount Everest, Marathon:** Landmark pages were not retrievable (403 or 404) or Britannica-only. The Eiffel summer-expansion claim has no figure on purpose. The Marathon origin is partly folklore-prone (1908 course, standardised by 1921), so confirm.
- **Pink-fairy, Marathon and Mount Everest:** 'Lives in' is approximate.
